# Top Tier Hauling: Nuxxion Sales quick guide

## 1. First login
1. Open the Nuxxion Sales address you were given. You see **Staff sign-in**.
2. Username **admin**, plus the temporary password you were given separately.
3. You are asked to **Set a permanent password**: enter the temporary one, a new one twice, press **Save password**. **(owner to confirm: that the forced change is on for this account.)**
4. You then land on the **Dashboard**.

## 2. New quote requests
Every quote request is a **Lead**. Open **Leads** in the menu. **(owner to confirm: that Top Tier's website quote form feeds this list.)**
- Each row shows name, email or phone, and a **stage** tag. New ones start at **New**.
- **Search** finds a name; **Mine** shows only yours.
- **New lead** adds one by hand (name required, plus email, phone or a note), e.g. a phone call.

## 3. Open a lead and change its status
1. Click the person's name in **Leads**.
2. Buttons **New**, **Contacted**, **Qualified** change the stage.
3. Edit details and press **Save**.
4. **Convert Lead** turns a qualified lead into a company, contact and opportunity. Afterwards the name, email, phone and note are locked; source, campaign and owner can still be changed. **(owner to confirm: whether to use Convert at all.)**

## 4. Notes
At the bottom of a lead, type under **Add note** and press **Add to history**. Notes keep their date and time.

## 5. Follow-up tasks
Open **Activities**. In **New activity** enter a description, type (Call, Email, Meeting, Task, Other), due date, and the **Lead**; press **Create activity**. Use the queue buttons (overdue, due today, upcoming) to see what is waiting. Press **Complete** when done. Tasks are made from Activities, not from the lead page.

## 6. Dashboard
Shown after login. **Work today** lists overdue and due-today activities. **Leads (current)** counts New, Contacted and Qualified. Other tiles cover activity queue, period results and lead sources. Pipeline and proposal figures stay at zero unless you record opportunities. **(owner to confirm: which tiles suit hauling.)**

## 7. If a quote doesn't appear
1. Refresh **Leads**; clear **Search** and untick **Mine**.
2. Try the customer's name or email spelled differently.
3. Still missing? **Call or email the owner** with the customer's name, phone or email, and when they sent it.
4. Meanwhile add it with **New lead**.
