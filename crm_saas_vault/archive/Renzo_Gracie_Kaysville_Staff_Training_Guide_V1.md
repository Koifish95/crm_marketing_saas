## How to start the day

**Purpose:** Know who to call and what marketing work is due.

**When:** Opening, or when you sit down at the desk.

**Steps:**

1. Log in.
2. **Dashboard** — new families and a snapshot of the funnel.
3. **Follow-up** — **phone calls** that are overdue or due today. This is acquisition work.
4. If you have Marketing access, **Marketing** — overdue **Marketing Tasks** (creative work), content waiting, events coming up.

**What happens afterward:** Nothing sends itself. You still call and you still post by hand.

**Common mistakes:** Mixing Follow-up with Marketing Tasks. They are two lists on purpose.

**Permissions:** Follow-up needs normal staff CRM access. Marketing needs Scott to grant View Marketing (and other roles for editing).

## How to work a household (Lead)

**Purpose:** One family (household) can have several people (adult, kids).

**When:** After a website Trial signup, a walk-in, or after an Event is processed.

**Steps:**

1. **Leads** → open the family.
2. The top is the **contact** (for kids, that is the parent/guardian).
3. Each **person** has their own program, intro, and join/lost.
4. Schedule a **Trial** (intro class) on the person who will step on the mat — not a fake parent line.
5. If they already came to an open house, that is **not** a Trial. You may still schedule an intro later, or **Convert** if they joined without an intro.

**What happens afterward:** Status history and notes stay. Completing a phone call does **not** mark them Joined.

**Common mistakes:** Deleting an intro to “reschedule.” Reschedule cancels the old visit and creates a new one. Both stay in history.

## How to do Follow-up (phone calls)

**Purpose:** Call the family. Assigned to whoever should call **this week**.

**When:** After a Trial is booked, after an Event is processed, or when you add a manual call.

**Steps:** Open **Follow-up** or the household. Assign if needed. Complete with an outcome (reached, voicemail, …). Cancel if the call should leave the queue.

**What happens afterward:** The family source/campaign does not change. Pay credit does not move to you just because you made the call.

**Permissions:** Staff CRM write.

## How to help with a Campaign (if assigned)

**Purpose:** A Campaign is a **plan** (kids trial month, open house push). Organic with $0 budget is normal. It is not “the Facebook ads manager.”

**When:** Scott created a campaign and asked you to use the tracking link or complete tasks.

**Steps:**

1. **Marketing → Campaigns** (if you can see it).
2. Copy the tracking link you were told to use. Do not invent a second campaign for a random post unless Scott wants it tracked.
3. **Marketing → Tasks** — your assigned creative work. Complete when done. Link on the task may open the content, photo, or event.

**Common mistakes:** Thinking you get paid because you are listed as collaborator. Collaborator is “who is helping,” not payroll.

## How to request or handle photos (Assets)

**Purpose:** Keep gym photos in one place and mark whether we should use them in ads/posts.

**When:** You shot the class, or a task says “request assets.”

**Steps:**

1. **Marketing → Assets** (needs Asset permission).
2. Upload. Leave use as unknown until Scott/you decide.
3. **Approve use** if it is fine for marketing.
4. **Restrict** or **Do not use** only with a **real reason** written in the box (whose kid, blur needed, etc.). Empty reason will be rejected.
5. This is **not** a legal waiver tracker. Paper/consent still lives outside the app.

**What happens afterward:** A “do not use” photo attached to a post record can block marking that content ready to publish.

## How to plan a post (Content)

**Purpose:** Write the caption, get approval if Scott requires it, post on Facebook/Instagram **yourself**, then record the link.

**When:** The work is part of a campaign, not a spontaneous story.

**Steps:**

1. **Marketing → Content.** Create title and caption. Check approval if Scott said so.
2. Use the buttons (needs assets, draft, approve, ready to publish). Do not expect the app to post.
3. **Planned manual publish time** is “I intend to post then,” not a Meta scheduler.
4. After you post, paste the public URL and **Record publication**. Pick Facebook vs Instagram if both were listed.

**Common mistakes:** Waiting for the app to publish. It will not.

**Permissions:** Manage content; Approve is a separate right.

## How to run an Event (open house / clinic)

**Purpose:** Collect signups on a roster, take attendance, then turn them into families to call.

**When:** Scott created the event. You have Event Manager and/or Event Processor.

### Before the door opens

1. Confirm sessions (times, capacity, ages) and **Publish**.
2. Public page is `/events/` plus the event name slug. Share a campaign tracking link if you want the campaign attached.
3. Registration can be closed by time window or **Close registration**. You can still add walk-ins as staff.

### At the event

1. Staff registration for walk-ins (phone required).
2. On the **roster**, set Attended / No-show / Cancelled. Event attendance is **not** an intro class.

### After the event — how to process an Event after it ends

**Purpose:** Create or match households and put a **call** on Follow-up.

**Steps:**

1. Open the Event.
2. Confirm attendance. Leave cancelled people cancelled unless Scott says to include them — then check **Include cancelled** on that row.
3. **Preview.** Read new vs existing vs **ambiguous**.
4. If two families share a phone, **pick** the household. The app will not guess.
5. **Process.** It is OK if you run it twice; already processed rows stay linked.

**What happens afterward:**

- A household and people exist (or were matched).
- A note that this was an Event, not a Trial.
- One Event follow-up **call** per family.
- **No Trial** until someone schedules an intro.

**Common mistakes:** Creating a fake Trial just to “have a class.” They can Join from Convert without an intro. Processing cancelled people without the checkbox.

**Permissions:** Process needs Event processor (or ADMIN).

## How to schedule a Trial after an Event

**Purpose:** Intro class for the person who will train.

**When:** They want a class time after the open house.

**Steps:** Open the household → the person → **Schedule trial**. Pick a listed intro slot. Reschedule if needed (old visit stays cancelled in history).

---

## How Join and Lost work

**Purpose:** Outcomes are **per person**, not “the whole family joined” unless there is only one person.

**Steps:** Convert with a membership offering, or Mark lost with a reason. Completing a call is not Join.
